    Click/drag to draw. Ship: click to place (greeps appear 30 px below it). Pile: click to add, drag to move, right-click or Delete to remove. Greeps step 5 px and only test the pixel they land on, so water thinner than ~6 px can be walked over ("thin" overlay). Keys: B L R E F S P V, [ ] size, X swap paint, Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y.

    Use: python -m policies.mlp_ppo.train --custom-maps maps, python -m tools.evaluate --maps maps.
